A little about me

Hi, I'm Zainab.

I'm a product thinker who enjoys turning messy problems into structured product decisions.

My approach is rooted in curiosity, empathy and practical execution. I like getting close to the problem, asking better questions, challenging assumptions and figuring out what is actually worth building.

I've worked on products spanning creator economy, health technology, AI-assisted experiences, communities and marketplaces — through real-world internship experience and self-initiated product work.

I'm particularly interested in products that sit at the intersection of people + technology, where understanding the human problem is just as important as understanding the technology.
